🏙️ MyCity AI — Complete Solution
One-line idea
MyCity AI is an AI-powered civic platform that allows citizens to report city problems through an app or WhatsApp, automatically understands and prioritizes those problems, helps authorities resolve them, and uses historical data to predict future civic hotspots.
👤 CITIZEN SIDE
1. 📸 Report a City Problem
What it does
Citizens can report problems such as:
Potholes
Garbage
Broken streetlights
Water leakage
Damaged roads
Drainage problems
Other civic issues

   ↓
Add description (optional)
   ↓
Location captured
   ↓
Submit
The citizen shouldn't have to fill a long complaint form.
Why it matters
Makes reporting quick and simple.

How?

Problem category

Why it matters
It prevents the authority dashboard from being filled with duplicate complaints.
